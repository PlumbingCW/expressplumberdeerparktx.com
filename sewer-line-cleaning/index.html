Main line clogged: stop the water now

A blocked main line gives clear warnings: a toilet that gurgles when the shower runs, a tub that fills when the washer drains, or a floor drain that bubbles. At that point, every gallon of water used in the house is going somewhere it should not.

Stop using water, locate your cleanout and call. We handle main line blockages 24/7. Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows.

What blocks a main line

Wipes are a leading cause of blockages on lines of every age. They do not dissolve, and they tangle with grease and roots into a plug that a cable can cut but not remove. Bin them, even if the package says flushable.

Does the city or does your plumber clear it?

Deer Park Public Works asks residents to call before hiring a plumber for a sewer problem. Crews inspect the sewer main in the street and clear a stoppage if it is there. If the main is clear, you are told to call a plumber. Their numbers are 281-478-7270 during business hours and 281-479-1511 after hours.

Make the call first and tell them what you see. If it turns out to be yours, we pick up the job. It saves money when the stoppage is on the city side.

Source note: flat ground and backups

The City of Deer Park describes the city as flat with poorly draining soils. A flat landscape means sewer mains run at gentle slopes, so any partial blockage slows flow quickly. After heavy rain, a line that is already partly blocked tends to back up first. Check your cleanout after a storm and hold off on laundry until the water recedes.

Keeping the main clear

Habits that protect your sewer line

  • ✓Throw wipes, paper towels and feminine products in the bin, not the toilet.
  • ✓Pour cooking grease into a can and put it in the trash.
  • ✓Know where your cleanout is and keep it uncovered and accessible.
  • ✓Schedule a camera inspection if you have had two or more main line clogs in a year. See sewer inspection.

After the line is clear

We run water through the line and watch the cleanout to confirm the flow is steady before we leave. If the camera showed roots, a belly or a cracked joint, we tell you what the likely return time is. A line with a damaged joint may clear for a few months and block again, while a line that was only greased can stay clear for years.

For rental properties and small businesses, a recurring cleaning schedule is cheaper than repeated emergency calls. See commercial plumbing for scheduled work on shops and multi-unit buildings.
