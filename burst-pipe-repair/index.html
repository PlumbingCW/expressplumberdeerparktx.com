When a burst pipe is an emergency

It almost always is. Pressure behind a failed pipe keeps pushing water into the house until the main valve is closed, so the first minutes matter more than anything we can do later.

Close the main valve, usually at the meter or where the line enters the house. Open a faucet at the lowest point to drain what is left in the line. We handle burst pipes 24/7 and book same-day appointments when available.

First five minutes after a pipe bursts

  • ✓Close the main water shutoff. If it will not turn, call the city's after-hours line at 281-479-1511.
  • ✓Switch off the breaker for any room with standing water, but only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely.
  • ✓Open the lowest faucet to relieve line pressure.
  • ✓Move valuables, rugs and electronics out of the water's path.
  • ✓Take photos and video for insurance before you start cleanup.

Why pipes burst in Gulf Coast homes

Age and corrosion

Galvanized and older copper pipe thins from the inside. The first sign is a pinhole, and the next is a split.

High water pressure

Pressure above what fixtures are rated for strains every joint. A regulator or a gauge test tells us whether it is too high.

Freezing weather

Hard freezes are rare on the coast but they happen. Pipes in exterior walls, attics and unheated garages are the usual victims.

Mechanical damage

A nail, a shovel or a fitting stressed by settling can crack a line without warning.

What a repair looks like

1

Find and open

We locate the failure, usually by following the water, and open the drywall or ceiling as little as needed.

2

Cut out the damage

The failed section is removed back to sound pipe.

3

Rebuild the line

We install new pipe in copper, PEX or CPVC and fittings matched to what is in your walls.

4

Test and restore

The line is pressure tested before it is closed up and the water is turned back on.

If the pipe is old and the failure is one of many pinholes, a patch is a short-term fix. We tell you when the pattern points to repiping instead of repairing the same line twice.

Water damage and what to do next

Water that sits in drywall and insulation can start mold within a day or two in Gulf Coast humidity. A plumber fixes the pipe. A water damage company dries the structure. We recommend calling your insurance agent early, because many policies cover sudden pipe failures and the resulting damage.

After the repair: keeping the next failure from happening

Once the line is back together, we check the water pressure at an outdoor spigot. Household pressure well above what fixtures are rated for strains every joint, and a failed regulator is a quiet cause of repeat leaks. We also test the main shutoff and any fixture stops we touched, so the next emergency has an easy first step.

If the break exposed old galvanized or brittle plastic pipe, we show you the condition of the pipe on either side of the repair. You decide whether to patch, replace a section or plan a larger project.
