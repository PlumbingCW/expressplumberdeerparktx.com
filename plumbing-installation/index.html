Venting: the part nobody sees

Every drain needs a vent that lets air in so water can flow. A fixture without proper venting gurgles, drains slowly and can siphon its trap dry, letting sewer gas into the home. Added bathrooms and kitchen islands are the most common venting trouble spots.

We plan vents before pipe is run. If you are adding a fixture, ask about venting at the first conversation, because it can decide where the fixture goes.

Permits for plumbing work in Deer Park

Source note: all plumbing work

The City of Deer Park describes its plumbing permit as covering all plumbing work, with water heaters, re-piping, sewer work and irrigation named as examples. A homeowner can pull a permit for work on a primary residence with a Harris County homestead exemption, but only the homeowner may do the work. If a contractor does the job, the contractor pulls the permit. Call the Permit Office at 281-479-2394 to confirm what applies to your project.

Choosing fixtures that last

Fixtures are bought on looks and installed on faith, but internal parts decide how long they last. Brass valve bodies and ceramic-disc cartridges outlast plastic internals in most households. Ask what the warranty covers and whether replacement parts will still be sold in ten years.

If you are remodeling a house that is decades old, plan to replace the supply stops and flex lines at the same time. A new faucet connected to a 40-year-old shutoff is a leak waiting to happen.
