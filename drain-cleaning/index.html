When a clogged drain is an emergency

Most slow drains can wait for a morning appointment. A drain that is backing up into the house cannot. If water is rising in a tub when the toilet flushes, if sewage smell is filling a room or if the washer discharge is spilling over a standpipe, treat it as urgent and call the emergency line.

Our dispatch is open nights, weekends and holidays, and we book same-day appointments when they are available. The longer wastewater sits on a slab-on-grade floor, the more of it reaches baseboards and carpet.

Do this while you wait for the plumber

  • ✓Stop running water. Skip the shower, the dishwasher and the washing machine until the line is clear.
  • ✓Do not pour chemical drain opener into a fully blocked line. It sits in the pipe and can burn the plumber's hands when the trap is opened.
  • ✓Move rugs, boxes and electronics off the floor near the affected drain.
  • ✓Locate the cleanout, usually a capped pipe in the yard or along the foundation, and keep the area clear for access.

Is the clog in one fixture or the whole line?

One fixture is slow

A single sink or tub that drains slowly points to a clog in that branch. Cabling the trap and branch usually clears it in a single visit.

Two or more fixtures back up

If the shower gurgles when the toilet flushes, the blockage is farther down the line, often at the main. That is a sewer line cleaning job.

Water comes up at the lowest drain

A floor drain or tub filling first usually means the main line is blocked downstream of the whole house.

It keeps coming back

A line that clogs every few months has a cause, such as roots, a belly or a damaged joint. A camera inspection finds it.

For anything beyond a simple cable job, we explain the choice before we run it. Hydro jetting costs more than cabling but it cleans the pipe wall instead of poking a hole through the stoppage, so it is the better value on greasy kitchen lines and repeat clogs.

Kitchen sinks, showers and floor drains

Kitchen drains clog from grease, coffee grounds and food that hardens as it cools in the trap and branch. Shower and tub drains clog from hair and soap scum that wrap around the stopper and drain strainer. Laundry standpipes clog from lint and overflow when the discharge outruns a half-blocked line.

Each of these is a different tool and a different length of cable. A slow kitchen sink that cables clear and returns within a month is the signal to jet it. A tub that only clears briefly may have a partial collapse downstream.

Older Deer Park lines and what the city handles

The city does not publish what its homes' drain lines are made of, so we find out with a camera. In general, cast iron and clay lines roughen with age and catch grease and roots more easily than modern PVC, which is one reason a line may clog more than once.

For the sewer main itself, the city asks residents to call Public Works first, at 281-478-7270 during business hours or 281-479-1511 after hours. Crews inspect the main and clear a stoppage on the city's side. If the problem is on your property, call us and we will clear the line and camera it.
