How to choose between services

If water is spreading, start with the shutoff and the emergency line, whatever the cause. If water is not spreading and you simply want to know what is wrong, describe the symptom on the phone and the plumber will point you to a service. A meter that never stops is a leak detection call, a gurgling toilet is a drain or sewer call and a cold shower is a water heater call.

For work you can plan, book ahead and compare options. A tankless conversion, a repipe or a remodel is easier to price when you are not standing in water.
