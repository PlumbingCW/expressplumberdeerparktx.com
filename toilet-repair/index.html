Toilet overflowing: act in this order

If water is rising in the bowl, lift the tank lid and push the flapper down, or close the supply valve behind the toilet by turning it clockwise. Both steps stop more water arriving. Do not flush again.

If the water is gray or smells like sewage, the problem may be farther down the line. See sewer line cleaning and call us. We handle toilet emergencies 24/7 and book same-day appointments when available.

Common toilet problems and the fix

Running water after a flush

A worn flapper, a flush valve seat or a fill valve that will not shut. Chlorinated water slowly hardens rubber parts, which is one reason flappers fail.

Weak flush

Mineral buildup in rim holes, a low water level in the tank or a partial clog. A clean and adjust often fixes it.

Rocking or leaking at the base

A failed wax ring or loose closet bolts. The toilet is lifted, the flange checked and a new ring set.

Tank leaking onto the floor

A cracked tank, loose tank bolts or a worn gasket. Not all of these are repairs.

Repair or replace the toilet?

Repair when

  • The porcelain is sound with no cracks
  • The fault is one inexpensive part such as a flapper or fill valve
  • The toilet flushes well when working
  • You like the model and finish

Replace when

  • The bowl or tank is cracked
  • The toilet is an old high-flow model that clogs often
  • Repairs keep stacking up
  • You want a taller, more water-saving or comfort-height model

If the toilet is a replacement, we remove the old one, check and repair the flange, set a new wax ring and test for leaks. Bring up any change to height or bowl shape on the call, so the right model is on the truck.

The flange: the repair behind the repair

The flange is the ring on the floor that holds the toilet to the drain. If it is cracked, corroded or sits below the finished floor, even a new wax ring will not seal. We check it on every base leak and can add a repair ring or replace the flange.

A leak at the base that goes unrepaired can soften the subfloor. Early repair is less costly than rebuilding the floor under the toilet.

What a toilet installation includes

  • ✓Shut off the supply, drain the old toilet and remove it.
  • ✓Check the flange and the floor for damage, and repair or replace the flange if needed.
  • ✓Set a new wax ring or seal and place the toilet level on the flange.
  • ✓Connect a new supply line, test the flush and check for leaks at the base and the tank.
  • ✓Haul away the old toilet unless you want to keep it.
