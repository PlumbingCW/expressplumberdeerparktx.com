Whose line is it? Sewer backups in Deer Park

A sewer backup has two possible sources. The line on your property is yours to fix. The city's main in the street is not. The City of Deer Park asks residents to call Public Works first when a main may be involved, at 281-478-7270 during business hours and 281-479-1511 after hours, because the city clears its own lines. If the city confirms its main is clear, the problem is on your side and a plumber takes over.

A quick sign that points to the main is several fixtures backing up at once, especially the lowest drains in the house. A single slow drain usually points to the line serving that fixture.
