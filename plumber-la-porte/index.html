Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

1

Shut off the main water

Find the main valve where the line enters the house, or at the meter box near the street, and turn it clockwise until it stops.

2

Kill power near water

If water is reaching outlets, appliances or the panel, switch the affected breakers off only if you can reach them while standing on dry floor.

3

Clear the immediate area

Move rugs, boxes and electronics. Keep kids and pets away from standing water, especially if sewage is involved.

4

Call the 24/7 line
