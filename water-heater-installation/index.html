Planned installation versus waiting for failure

A water heater installed on your schedule costs less stress than one installed at midnight. You can choose the size, compare fuel types and get the permit in order. If your tank is already leaking, skip to emergency water heater replacement and we will treat it as urgent.

Our 24/7 line takes planning calls too. Describe the home, the current heater and what you want to change, and a plumber will outline the options.

Choosing size and fuel

Household size

A 40-gallon tank suits many two- to three-person homes. Larger families, soaking tubs or multiple bathrooms may want 50 gallons or more.

Gas or electric

Existing fuel service usually decides it. Switching adds line or circuit work, so we price it separately.

Hybrid heat pump

Uses far less electricity per gallon but needs open space and a condensate drain. Verify the room before buying.

Tankless

Frees floor space and provides continuous hot water, but needs adequate gas or electrical capacity. See tankless water heater.

What a proper installation includes

Standard parts of a code-minded install

  • ✓A new shutoff valve on the cold inlet, so the heater can be isolated in the future.
  • ✓New flexible water connectors and, for gas units, a new gas connector and a leak test.
  • ✓A temperature and pressure relief valve with a discharge line routed to a safe location.
  • ✓A drain pan and drain line where a leak would damage finished space.
  • ✓An expansion tank when the system is closed and thermal expansion is a concern.

We also verify venting on gas models. A flue that draws poorly can send combustion gas into the home, so we check the draft before we leave.

Sizing a tank by how you use hot water

Tank size matters less than how much hot water you draw in the busiest hour. Two showers back to back, a dishwasher cycle and a load of laundry can empty a small tank before the last person is rinsed. Tell the plumber who lives in the house, when the heaviest use falls and whether you have a soaking tub or a second bathroom.

A tank that is too large wastes energy holding hot water. One that is too small leaves someone cold. We size to your pattern, and we explain why when we recommend going up or down a size.

Scheduling, permits and the city

The City of Deer Park requires a plumbing permit for water heaters. You can apply online through the city's Citizen Self Service portal or at the Public Works Permit Office at City Hall. We pull the permit for installations and coordinate any required inspection.

Effective July 15, 2025, the city adopted the 2024 International Building Code and the 2023 National Electrical Code. The city's page does not name a plumbing code edition, so we confirm the current local requirement with Public Works rather than quoting a code year.

Older homes and surprises behind the heater

Heater closets in older houses can be tight. Corroded unions and galvanized nipples, where a house has them, can break when disturbed, which is why a plumber should plan for replacement of the connecting pieces.

If the supply lines show heavy corrosion, we discuss repiping options rather than patching the next fitting that fails.
