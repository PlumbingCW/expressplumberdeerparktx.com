When to call the city first

City of Deer Park Public Works

If you think the problem is on the city's side of the meter, such as a water main leak or a sewer main backup, the city asks residents to report it to Public Works at 281-478-7270 during business hours and 281-479-1511 after hours. The city's guidance on sewer trouble is to call Public Works before a plumber, because the city clears the main and a plumber handles the line on your side. If you are not sure which side the problem is on, call us and we will help you sort it out.
