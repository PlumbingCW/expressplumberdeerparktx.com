Slab leak signs that need a call tonight

A slab leak is a break in a pressurized water line under the foundation. Because the water is under pressure, it keeps flowing until someone shuts it off. A leak that wets the subfloor or the soil beneath a slab can undermine flooring and invite mold.

Call the emergency line when you see water at the floor with no obvious source, when the floor feels warm or when you hear water running at night. We dispatch same-day when appointments are available.

A hot spot on the floor

A leaking hot line warms the slab above it. Barefoot is the old test, and a thermal camera is the better one.

Water sound with everything off

A hiss or trickle in a quiet house points to a pressurized line.

A meter that drifts

With all fixtures off and the main valve open, a moving meter means water is escaping.

Low pressure or a cracked tile line

Dropping pressure at faucets or a hairline crack that follows a line in the floor can mark a leak below.

What soil and age mean for Deer Park slabs

The City of Deer Park marks its oldest home as dating to 1928, but it does not publish the age or pipe material of its housing. In general, copper and galvanized steel corrode and pit with age, and copper in contact with concrete or wet soil can develop pinhole leaks over time.

The City of Deer Park describes the area as flat with poorly draining soils. Water from a slow leak stays near the pipe instead of draining away, which keeps pitting active and can hide the leak for months.

Source note: groundwater and ground movement

The Harris-Galveston Subsidence District explains that long-term groundwater pumping compacts clay and silt layers and causes land to sink across the region. Its brochure ties that to flooding and infrastructure strain, but does not name pipes or foundations specifically. A plumber cannot tell you whether movement caused your leak, only where it is and how to fix it.

While you wait for the plumber

  • ✓Close the main valve and confirm the meter stops moving.
  • ✓Move rugs and furniture away from any damp flooring so it can start to dry.
  • ✓Turn on a few faucets to drain the lines and reduce the water left to escape.
  • ✓Photograph damp floors, stains and the meter dial for your insurance claim.

After the repair we recommend a day or two of airflow and, if flooring was wet, a moisture reading before you replace anything. Sealing wet carpet or hardwood too early traps moisture and invites mold. A water damage company can dry the structure if the area is large.
