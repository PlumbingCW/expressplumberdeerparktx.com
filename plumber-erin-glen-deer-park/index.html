About Erin Glen in Deer Park

The City of Deer Park's subdivision index lists Erin Glen in nine numbered sections, with two of them split into 6a and 6b. Erin Glen Court is one of the streets that carries the name. Larger homes and hot water demand

A median home near 1,959 square feet is larger than the medians for Park Meadows, Deer Meadows or Deer Park Gardens in the same data, and larger homes often have more bathrooms drawing on the same heater. That is a general pattern and not a promise about any house. If showers run cold when two bathrooms are in use, the heater may be undersized or failing, and a plumber can check capacity and condition.

A tank stores and keeps hot water ready. A tankless unit heats it as it flows. Tankless suits many households but needs correct sizing, venting and in many cases a gas line upgrade, so the quote should cover all of it.

Permits and hardness

The City of Deer Park requires a plumbing permit for water heater work. The city's published water quality information lists chlorine as its disinfectant and does not report water hardness, so we do not state a hardness figure here. If hard water worries you, a water test from a lab or a softener dealer will give you an actual number for your tap.
