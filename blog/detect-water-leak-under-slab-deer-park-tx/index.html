How to Detect a Water Leak Under a Slab in Deer Park, TX

The fastest way to detect a water leak under a slab is the meter test. Turn off every fixture, read the water meter and watch the low-flow dial. If it moves, water is escaping somewhere. Then close the main valve at the house. If the dial stops, the leak is inside the house or under the slab. If it keeps moving, the leak is on the line outside.

The meter test, step by step

1

Stop all water use

Close taps, pause the washer and dishwasher and make sure no toilet is running.

2

Find the meter

It sits in a box near the street or the property line. Lift the lid carefully and check for insects first.

3

Watch the leak indicator

Most meters have a small triangle or dial that spins at any flow. If it turns, water is moving.

4

Close the house valve

Turn the main shutoff at the house and look again. A stopped dial means the leak is on the house side of the valve.

Write down the meter reading, wait an hour with no use and read it again. A change confirms the leak even if the dial is hard to see. Take photos of both readings. They help the plumber and any insurance claim.

Ruling out the easy causes first

Check these before you assume the slab

  • ✓Look under every sink and behind every toilet for a slow drip.
  • ✓Check the water heater closet for dampness and a dripping relief valve.
  • ✓Look at outdoor spigots and any irrigation valves for continuous flow.
  • ✓Fill a toilet tank with food coloring to check for a silent flapper leak.
  • ✓Inspect ceilings and walls on the lowest floor for stains.

Fixture leaks are far more common than slab leaks. A running toilet can mimic a slab leak on a meter. The checks above take half an hour and can save a plumbing visit.

What professionals use that you cannot

Acoustic ground microphones

A sensitive listening device picks up the sound a leak makes through concrete and soil, then a technician maps the loudest point.

Thermal cameras

A hot line leaking under the slab warms the surface above it, which a thermal camera shows as a bright patch.

Pressure testing

Sections of pipe are isolated and tested. A pressure drop identifies the failing section.

Moisture meters

They show how far water has spread in flooring, baseboards and the lower wall.

With these tools a plumber can often pin a leak to a patch of floor a few feet across. That is the difference between a small, targeted opening and breaking a whole room. See slab leak repair for the repair options and leak detection for the locating methods.

If the meter keeps moving after the house valve is closed

City side versus your side

If the meter still moves with the house valve closed, the leak is between the meter and the valve, or on the city's side. The City of Deer Park asks residents to report water leaks to Public Works at 281-478-7270 during business hours and 281-479-1511 after hours. The city's FAQ lists those numbers for leak reports, so a call there is the right first step for a leak at or before the meter.

Source type: City of Deer Park Public Works FAQ

The ground matters too. The city describes Deer Park as flat, with poorly draining soils. A leak that cannot drain away tends to saturate the soil near the pipe. A wet patch or sunken spot near the house after a dry spell is worth a call.

What to do while you wait for a plumber

Limit the damage

  • ✓Close the main valve at the house if you cannot tell where the water is going.
  • ✓Move rugs, boxes and furniture off any damp floor and keep the area ventilated.
  • ✓Take dated photos of the meter, the damp spots and any stains.
  • ✓Do not drill or chisel into the slab. A cut line or a wire in the concrete is a bigger problem than a leak.
  • ✓Write down when the meter was read and how fast the dial moved so the plumber can size the leak.

A slow leak can run for weeks before a bill shows it. A fast one can soak a floor in hours. Either way, shutting off the main buys time without costing anything.
