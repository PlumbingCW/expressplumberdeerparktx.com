What a CCTV inspection actually is

A CCTV sewer inspection sends a waterproof camera on a push cable through your sewer lateral, from a cleanout or a pulled toilet, to the connection at the street. The plumber watches live video and records it. The recording shows pipe material, joints, cracks, roots, bellies and buildup.

A locator in the camera head sends a signal that can be traced above ground. That lets the plumber mark the depth and position of any problem, which matters if a repair follows.

A fair way to compare quotes

Questions to ask every plumber

  • ✓Is the price for the camera run only, or does it include cleaning, a locate and a written report?
  • ✓Will I receive the video file and a summary I can share with a buyer or contractor?
  • ✓Does the plumber hold a current Texas license, and what is the number?
  • ✓If the camera finds damage, is the inspection fee credited toward the repair?
  • ✓Is there a trip charge or an after-hours charge on top of the quoted price?

The lowest quote is not always the lowest cost. A cheap inspection that stops at the first obstruction, or comes without a recording, leaves you without the information you wanted. Ask for the scope in writing or in a text message.

When an inspection pays for itself

Before closing on a house

The camera can show roots, a collapsed section or a belly before the purchase is final.

After repeated clogs

A line that blocks twice a year has a cause. The camera finds it so you stop paying for repeated cleanings.

Before an expensive repair

Video lets you confirm the damage and compare repair methods. See sewer line repair.

When a yard is wet or sinking

A camera can confirm whether the line is leaking into the soil.

Who handles what in Deer Park

City main versus your lateral

The City of Deer Park's Public Works guidance says that for sewer problems residents should call the department first. Crews inspect the sewer main, and if the blockage is in the main, the city clears it. If the problem is on your side, you are told to call a plumber. The numbers are 281-478-7270 in business hours and 281-479-1511 after hours. A private camera inspection covers your lateral and the connection, not the street main.

Source type: City of Deer Park Public Works FAQ

The oldest home the city marks dates to 1928, but the city does not publish the age or material of its laterals. Older laterals may be clay or cast iron, and a camera is the only way to see their condition. See sewer inspection for how we run one.

What a good report contains

Items to expect

  • ✓The pipe material and approximate diameter.
  • ✓The distance from the entry point to each finding.
  • ✓Photos or timestamps of roots, cracks, bellies and offsets.
  • ✓A plain summary of whether the line is sound, needs cleaning or needs repair.
  • ✓The recording itself, in a format you can share.

A report like this is useful in a home sale, in a landlord dispute or when you compare repair bids. Several contractors can then price the same defect, which makes the comparison fair.
