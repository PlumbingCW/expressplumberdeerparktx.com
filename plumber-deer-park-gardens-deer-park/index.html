Water heaters, fixtures and the permit question

A home of this size usually has one water heater serving a handful of bathrooms and a kitchen. When it fails or starts to leak, the question is usually repair or replace. Age of the tank, the cost of the repair and whether the leak is at a fitting or in the tank itself all matter. A leak from the tank body means replacement, while a failed part may be repairable.

Deer Park requires a plumbing permit for all plumbing work, and a water heater replacement is plumbing work. Ask whoever quotes the job how the permit will be handled. Work done without a permit can bring a $500 penalty plus triple the permit fee under the city's rules.

Check the plumber's license

Texas licenses plumbers through the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners. You can look up any plumber on the board's public license search, or call the board at (512) 936-5200. A plumber who gives a name and license number when asked is easy to verify, and that is worth doing before any water heater or repiping job.

Source type: Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners and City of Deer Park contractor pages

Signs a water heater needs attention

Check these

  • ✓Water around the base of the tank, which means shut off the cold supply and call.
  • ✓Rust-colored hot water or a rumbling noise when it heats.
  • ✓A relief valve that drips or a discharge pipe that runs.
  • ✓Water that runs lukewarm or runs out faster than it used to.

If you smell gas near a gas water heater, leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. See also the city page for plumber in Deer Park.
