Deer Park, TX: our home service area

Deer Park is in Harris County, covers about 10 square miles and uses ZIP code 77536. Our office is at 3709 Center Street, near the Shoppes at Town Square and not far from City Hall, the public library and Clyde Abshier Stadium.

From there we reach homes and businesses along Spencer Highway and Highway 225, and the older streets on either side of Center Street. See the emergency plumber page for Deer Park for the full list of services.

La Porte, TX: our east-side area

La Porte uses ZIP 77571 and a PO box ZIP, 77572. It has its own page with ZIP codes, local notes and an emergency preparation checklist. Visit emergency plumber in La Porte for more.

How we decide whether to dispatch

We use driving distance, not a radius on a map. Highways, rail and the Houston Ship Channel all change the real travel time, and a plumber who has to cross a bridge or wait at a train crossing arrives later than the mileage suggests. That is why we ask for your address at the start of every call.
