A jammed or leaking disposal: safe first steps

Never put your hand in a disposal, even when the power is off at the switch. Turn off the wall switch and, for a hardwired unit, the breaker. Then look under the sink. A puddle there means a leak, and a hum from the unit with no spinning means a jam.

We take disposal emergencies 24/7 and book same-day appointments when available. A leak under the sink can ruin a cabinet floor quickly, so shut the cold stop valve and dry the area while you wait.

What each symptom usually means

Humming and no spin

A jammed flywheel. A hex key in the bottom socket often frees it, and the red reset button on the base restores power after.

No sound at all

A tripped reset, a failed switch or a dead motor. Plumbers test the circuit before replacing the unit.

Leak from the bottom

A cracked housing or failed seal. This is a replacement, not a repair.

Leak at the top or side

A loose sink flange, a worn gasket or a loose drain connection. Often a repair.

Why disposals clog the sink drain

A disposal grinds food into pieces that must travel through the sink trap and the branch line. Grease, coffee grounds and starchy food like rice and pasta can settle in that line and form a plug downstream of the unit. A sink that backs up with the disposal running is a drain cleaning problem, not a disposal fault.

Run cold water for 15 seconds after every use, avoid pouring fat down the sink and feed waste in small amounts. These habits protect the line as well as the blades.

Installing a new disposal

1

Disconnect the old unit

We shut the water, unplug or disconnect power, loosen the mounting ring and lower the old disposal out.

2

Mount the new one

The sink flange is sealed and the new unit is locked to the mounting ring so it hangs level.

3

Open the dishwasher inlet

New disposals ship with a knockout plug in the dishwasher port. If the plug stays in, the dishwasher will not drain, so we remove it.

4

Connect, test and run

The drain, dishwasher hose and power are reconnected. We run water and the unit and check every joint.
