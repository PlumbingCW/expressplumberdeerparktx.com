After-hours plumbing emergencies for businesses

For a business, a plumbing failure means lost sales, a health code risk and sometimes a closed door. Our 24/7 line takes commercial calls the same way it takes residential ones, and a licensed plumber helps you decide what to shut off and who to notify.

If water is near electrical panels, equipment or customers, close the building's main shutoff and keep people out of the area. Then call us. We dispatch same-day when appointments are available.

Leak detection and drain work for commercial properties

Commercial leaks are harder to trace because pipes run through suspended ceilings, shared walls and slab sections under tenant spaces. We use acoustic listening, thermal imaging and pressure tests, the same methods described under leak detection, and we plan after-hours testing when the space is empty.

Floor drain overflows and lift pump failures are calls we plan for. After a heavy rain, we check floor drains and any pumps before water backs up into the building.

Registration, permits and insurance in Deer Park

Source note: contractor requirements

The City of Deer Park requires plumbing contractors to register before pulling permits. Registration is free. The city requires proof of general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, with limits of at least $300,000 for bodily injury and $300,000 for property damage per occurrence, from an insurer rated A or A+ by A.M. Best. If you hire a plumber for a commercial job, ask for the registration and a certificate of insurance, and verify the license on the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners public search.
