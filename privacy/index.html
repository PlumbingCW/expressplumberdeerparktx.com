Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, the address you give us and what you tell us about the problem. We use that to dispatch a plumber, to follow up on the job and to improve how we answer calls.

What this website collects

This website has no forms, accounts or comment fields, so we do not ask you to type personal details into it. Like most sites, our hosting provider may keep basic server logs, such as the pages requested and the time of the request, to keep the site running and secure.

Email

If you email contact@expressplumberdeerparktx.com, we receive the address you send from and the message itself. We use it to answer you. Please do not send payment card numbers or other sensitive details by email.

Sharing

We share your details with the plumber assigned to your job so the visit can happen. We may also share information when the law requires it. We do not use this page to make claims about selling data, and we do not describe any arrangement here that we have not confirmed.

Your choices

If you would rather not be recorded, tell the person who answers at the start of the call, and we will discuss what we can do. You can also email us to ask what we hold about you or to ask us to delete it, and we will respond as the law requires.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.

Questions about this policy can go to contact@expressplumberdeerparktx.com, or you can call (281) 626-9205. Our address is 3709 Center St, Deer Park, TX 77536. See also our terms of use.
