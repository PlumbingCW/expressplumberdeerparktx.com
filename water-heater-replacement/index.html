Tank failed: what to do before we arrive

When a water heater fails, the problem is water, not just heat. A tank that has corroded through can empty its contents across a garage or utility room floor in minutes. Use the cold inlet valve above the tank to stop the refill.

Then cut the power at the breaker for an electric unit, or turn the gas control to its off or pilot position. If you smell gas, leave the home and call 911 before calling a plumber. We handle emergency replacement 24/7 and book same-day appointments when available.

First steps for a failed water heater

  • ✓Close the cold water inlet valve on top of the heater.
  • ✓Switch off the breaker (electric) or turn the gas control off (gas).
  • ✓Open a hot tap in the house to relieve pressure and prevent the tank from refilling.
  • ✓Move belongings off the floor and sweep standing water toward a floor drain if one is nearby.

What replacement day looks like

1

Drain and disconnect

We shut off supply and gas or power, drain the old tank and disconnect the lines.

2

Set and connect

The new unit goes on a stand or pan, water lines are reconnected with new flex connectors and a relief valve is installed with a proper discharge line.

3

Vent, expansion and gas

Gas units get a vent check and a leak test on the gas connection. We discuss an expansion tank if your system calls for one.

4

Permit and cleanup

We pull the City of Deer Park plumbing permit, haul away the old tank and show you the shutoffs.

Permit, inspection and code in Deer Park

Deer Park's Public Works Permit Office at City Hall, 710 E. San Augustine, issues plumbing permits for water heaters. The office is open Monday through Thursday 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. and Friday 7:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m., and can be reached at 281-479-2394.

Skipping the permit risks a $500 penalty and triple the permit fee. A permitted job also gives your insurer and any future buyer a record that the replacement was done to local requirements.

Source note: contractor registration

The city requires contractors to register, with proof of liability insurance, before pulling plumbing permits. That is a reason to ask any plumber you hire for a license number you can check with the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners.

How old homes change a water heater swap

Tight heater closets, old gas flues and worn shutoff valves can all complicate a swap that looks routine, and they are more common the longer a house has stood.

On any older home we check the shutoff valve before we touch anything, because a valve that will not close is a bigger problem than a tank that will not heat. We replace it first if needed. That keeps the replacement from turning into a flood.
